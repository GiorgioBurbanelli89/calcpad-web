Finite Element Analysis of Rectangular Plate — Mindlin-Reissner Theory

Theory

The Mindlin-Reissner plate theory accounts for transverse shear deformation,

unlike Kirchhoff theory which assumes zero shear strain.

Each node has 3 DOFs: vertical displacement w, and rotations θₓ, θᵧ.


This formulation is suitable for thick plates where t/a > 1/10.

and K_s uses 1-point reduced integration (shear, to avoid locking)
