Propped Cantilever with Concentrated Moment

Input data

Beam Length - l = 10  m

Load - M = 10  kN·m

Distances - a = 6  m,

b = l − a = 10 − 6 = 4 m, k = bl = 410 = 0.4

Internal forces

Bending at support

MA = M ·  ( 1 − 3 · k2 ) 2 = 10 ·  ( 1 − 3 · 0.42 ) 2 = 2.6 kN·m

Shear

V = 3 · M ·  ( 1 − k2 ) 2 · l = 3 · 10 ·  ( 1 − 0.42 ) 2 · 10 = 1.26 kN

Bending at mid-span

Ma = V · a − MA = 1.26 · 6 − 2.6 = 4.96 kN·m

Mb = V · b = 1.26 · 4 = 5.04 kN·m
